Unseen Browser
Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 9 November 2026

About this policy

This policy describes how Unseen Browser (Android package com.hiddenlantern.unseen) handles information. Unseen Browser is provided by Hidden Lantern. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to Hidden Lantern. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to nova.devsup@gmail.com. Unseen Browser is independent of DuckDuckGo, Google, Meta, and the websites you visit. You do not need an Unseen Browser account to browse.

Browsing, search, and websites

When you open a page, your device connects to that website and its content providers. They receive information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address, requested address, browser information, and any cookies or information you submit. Searches submitted through the address bar are sent to Google. Address-bar suggestions are generated locally rather than by sending unfinished queries to a remote suggestion service.

Logging in, posting, uploading, messaging, and making purchases on websites send information to those services. Their privacy policies apply. Website ads and analytics may still operate despite blocking features. We do not receive a copy of your browsing history, search history, social messages, or passwords as part of ordinary browsing.

Information stored on your device

The app processes and can store tabs, browsing history, bookmarks, site permissions, settings, cached pages, cookies, website storage, downloaded files, and saved login information to provide browser features. Locally stored information is subject to your browsing mode and clearing settings. Bookmarks, passwords, and files you deliberately save can remain after a browsing session ends.

Importing bookmarks or passwords reads the file or source you select and adds the selected information to local browser storage. Exported files are placed where you choose; exported password files can contain readable credentials. Protect or delete these files when you no longer need them. When you choose a saved password, it is supplied to the selected website to complete sign-in. Android device authentication is requested by the social password picker.

Facebook and Instagram sessions

The Social Browser loads the actual Facebook and Instagram websites. Their accounts, messages, cookies, and privacy choices remain governed by those services. The Social Browser uses a separate WebView data directory from ordinary browser pages. Social cookies and website storage can persist between app launches. Cookies, including third-party cookies, are allowed in that social directory for website functionality.

Clearing ordinary browser data does not clear social sessions. Use the separate Clear social sessions option in Social Browser settings to remove social cookies and website storage and sign out. This does not delete your Facebook or Instagram account, information already held by those services, or passwords saved separately in the browser.

Protection features and network requests

The app uses local rules and scripts to reduce selected trackers, ads, and social activity signals and to support HTTPS upgrades. Protection configuration, tracker lists, replacement resources, and security datasets can be downloaded from upstream providers, including DuckDuckGo and their content-delivery providers. Those providers receive the network information needed to handle these requests, including your IP address and request headers.

When malicious-site protection is active, checks can send a partial cryptographic hash derived from a site indicator to DuckDuckGo to retrieve potential matches. This is a protection request, separate from app analytics. Protection features are not a guarantee that all advertising, tracking, malicious content, read receipts, or activity indicators will be blocked. They do not make you anonymous or hide activity from a service you are logged into.

Permissions and optional device features

Internet access is needed to load websites and protection updates. Camera, microphone, and location permissions support features you choose, such as website camera access, recording, or sharing your location. Files and photos you select can be supplied to a website when you choose to upload them. Microphone access can also support voice input; the Android speech or keyboard service you use may have its own privacy practices. Permissions can be reviewed or revoked in Android settings.

Notification access supports browser notifications and download status. File and media access supports selected imports, uploads, exports, and downloads. Android’s launcher handles home-screen shortcuts and widgets, whose names and icons are visible to people who can view your home screen.

Where device-app search or App Tracking Protection is available and used, the app can inspect installed application names and package identifiers for local app lookup and protection controls. Optional App Tracking Protection uses Android’s local VPN interface to examine connection information, associate connections with apps, and block known tracker destinations. This local filtering is not a remote anonymity VPN.

Analytics, advertising, and disabled services

The current release has no developer-served advertisements, AdMob integration, or in-app purchases. It does not use developer-operated app analytics, automatic app crash reporting, or a developer-integrated advertising SDK. The upstream app telemetry, promotional campaigns, Duck.ai integration, native cloud sync, and subscription offers have been disabled. We do not sell personal information or use your browsing data to build advertising profiles.

This does not prevent websites, Android, Google Play, the WebView provider, your keyboard, or other apps from processing information under their own settings and policies. In particular, websites opened in the browser may display their own advertisements and use analytics. Information you voluntarily send to us for support is handled as described below.

Feedback and support

Feedback is optional. The guided form prepares an email containing your chosen category, any selected detail, and the description you provide, addressed to nova.devsup@gmail.com. It opens your email app for you to review and send. We receive it only if you send it. Your email app can include your sender address, display name, and attachments you add. The guided form does not automatically attach your browsing history, saved passwords, screenshots, or a list of visited websites.

We use support information to answer you, investigate the issue you report, and improve the app. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, financial details, or other sensitive information. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store it.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Information goes to the websites and services you choose to use, providers needed for protection updates or security checks, Android services involved in the features you use, and our email provider if you contact us. We may disclose information actually held by us when required by applicable law or necessary to establish or defend legal claims. We do not have a developer-side database of your ordinary browsing history or locally saved passwords to disclose.

Local data remains until removed by the relevant browser settings, session rules, storage management, or Android app-data clearing. Review the clearing options: clearing browsing data does not necessarily remove saved passwords, bookmarks, downloaded files, or separate social sessions. Deleting an item from the browser does not delete a file you exported elsewhere or information already sent to a website. Android Settings > Apps > Unseen Browser > Storage > Clear storage removes the app’s local data; downloaded or exported files outside app-private storage must be deleted separately.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request, resolve related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence we hold by emailing the privacy contact. Third-party websites, email services, and hosting providers control their own retention and deletion practices. Delete third-party accounts through the relevant provider.

Security and international processing

Android app isolation and the browser’s credential controls help protect local information. HTTPS encrypts supported connections in transit, but an HTTP website can be unencrypted. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device, lock screen, and exported files.

Websites, protection providers, email providers, and the hosting provider for this policy may process information in countries other than yours. Their policies describe their handling and applicable safeguards. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, or obtain a copy of personal information we actually hold. You may withdraw permission through Android settings and contact us about privacy rights. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Withdrawing permission does not undo information already sent to a website.

Children

Unseen Browser is a general-purpose browser and is not designed specifically for children. Websites have their own age restrictions. We do not intentionally request children’s personal information for developer-operated services. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Relevant provider policies: Google, Meta, DuckDuckGo, and GitHub. Other websites you visit provide their own notices.
